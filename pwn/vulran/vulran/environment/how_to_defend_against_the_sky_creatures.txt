Document Name: How To Defend Against The Sky Creatures
Classification: Public
Date: 4th August 2045

Dear Citizens of Vulran,

It has come to our attention that the flying anomalies have been spotted significantly
more often in the past few weeks. These anomalies are widely known as "sky creatures".

The Vulran National University (VNU) and the Vulran Government had conducted a set of
thorough investigations of these creatures. The Astral Ministry concluded that these
creatures are very likely more technologically advanced than us humans.

Due to the significant number of injuries and deaths caused by sky creatures, we have
developed a short set of guidelines to defend against the sky creatures in case of any 
encounters.

1. When you encounter a creature, draw your distance. They emit hypnotic gases, and our
experiments show that organisms exposed to the gas show symptoms such as paranoia, confusion,
and nausea. In extreme cases, the gas can cause patients to fall into deep comas, or
could even be fatal. 

2. Never look away from sky creatures. They seem to fear human eyesight, even when drawing
distance, we recommend walking backwards while staring at them intensely. 

3. Do not engage. Sky creatures may exert forces hundreds of times more powerful than a human.

4. Lay flat, play dead. Unlike earthly animals, sky creatures rely only on sight. They cannot
smell, hear, or touch. If you appear dead, they won't approach you.

While these guidelines come from experimental and anecdotal reports, we have the brightest
scientists studying these beings, and we will announce an update as soon as new findings
are made.

We understand these creatures have been causing significant worries in the community, and
we'd like to assure you that we are here to support your.

We wish you best of luck with your everyday lives, and stay safe.

Sincerely,
Joe Barrondoof,
General Secretary of Vulran
