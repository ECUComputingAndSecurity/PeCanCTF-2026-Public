Document Name: Last Words of Mary Finbell
Classification: Public
Date: 3rd December 2052

My name is Mary Finbell, this is my final message to the world.

Who would've thought, a lonely woman can only do so much for the world...
The creatures keep coming, this pungent gas only gets stronger as days go by.
I've rid close to a hundred of these beings from their pathetic
existence, but I can only do so much. I write this to the world with several broken ribs,
fading closer to my demise. If I had the energy to kill one more sky creature, I would.
But I think it's time for my duty to come to an end. 

This, however, is only the beginning. You don't have to be a soldier to fight, you only 
need courage. I hereby call for every citizen of Vulran to stand for your home, and fight
to the very end. Until then, there's no defeat, no victory for these unearthly aliens. 

My name is Mary Finbell, I am no one special, just a fighter for my homeland.

Fairwell.
